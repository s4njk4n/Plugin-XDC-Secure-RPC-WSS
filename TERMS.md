These terms cover the paid services described in the [README](README.md#done-for-you): the guided install, the done-for-you install, the node care plan and ad-hoc help. The installer scripts themselves are free and open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

| Service | Price | Included |

| Guided install | US$90 | A live screen-share while you run the installer, until both endpoints work. |

## What isn't included

- The server, domain and their costs. You rent and own them.
- Outages or problems at your hosting provider, domain registrar or Cloudflare.
- XDC network-wide issues, client bugs, or changes in XDC Network software.
- Problems caused by changes you or someone else makes to the server after handover.
- 24/7 or emergency response. Replies are best-effort within 24 hours.

## Payment

- Prices are in US dollars, payable in the US-dollar equivalent in XDC, USDT or AUDD at the time of payment.
- Installs are paid upfront. Care plans are paid monthly in advance, and you can stop at any time; the plan runs to the end of the paid month.
- If I can't get your endpoints working on a done-for-you or guided install, you pay nothing, or you get a full refund. Once the endpoints work and are handed over, the install fee isn't refundable.

## Access and security

- You create a temporary user on your server and add my SSH key. I never ask for your root password, seed phrases, private keys or wallet access. An RPC node doesn't need any of these.
- I remove my access at handover, and you can confirm it's gone. Care plan customers decide how access is handled during the plan.

## Privacy

I keep only what's needed to do the work: your contact details, your server's IP address and domain, and the IPs you whitelist. I don't share them with anyone unless the law requires it, and I delete them when the work or plan ends.
